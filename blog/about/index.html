About this blog

Who writes it, how it's made, and the standards every post has to meet.

CC

Crhistian C · Founder of Rep Rot

Crhistian builds Rep Rot, an iPhone app that locks the apps you lose time to until you do your reps. This blog shares what actually helps: the research on screen time and habits, and simple bodyweight training anyone can start today.

How posts are made

Posts are researched and drafted with the help of AI, then checked against our standards before they go up. Most illustrations are AI-generated; a few use photos from the Rep Rot app. Crhistian is responsible for everything published here.

Our standards

  • Sources for every claim. Studies, official guidance and Apple's own support pages, linked at the end of each post. If we can't verify something, we leave it out.
  • Honest about evidence. We say how big a study was and whether it shows cause or just a link.
  • Safety first. Exercise advice is conservative, with easier versions for beginners. No medical, diet or weight-loss promises, and no body-shaming.
  • Straight about Rep Rot. We only describe what the app actually does, and we never pay for or invent reviews or testimonials.
  • Credit where it's due. When a post is inspired by someone's video or idea, we name and link them.

Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, email help@reprotapp.com. We fix errors quickly and update the post's date.

Not medical advice

Everything here is general information. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, especially with a health condition, an injury or during pregnancy. If you're struggling with your mental health, please reach out to someone you trust or a local support line.
