Post: https://reprotapp.com/blog/how-long-to-hold-a-plank/

Notes: no abs, belly or appearance hooks. The McGill study is 24 young men
measuring torso stiffness, not plank time; never say it "proved" 10 seconds

## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** You held a one-minute plank. The last 15 seconds were your lower back.
2. **The rule:** Hold only as long as you keep a straight line and breathe normally. When form breaks, the set is over.
3. **By level:** New: 3 × 10–20 s on your knees. Some training: 2–3 × 20–30 s. Comfortable: 2–3 × 30–60 s.
4. **Short beats long:** Several clean holds give you more good seconds than one long hold that falls apart.
5. **When to stop:** Hips sag or pike. You feel it in your lower back. Shoulders creep up. You're holding your breath.
6. **Save this ladder:** Wall → counter → knees → forearms → straight arms. Move up at 3 steady holds of 20–30 s.
7. **CTA:** Past 60 clean seconds, make it harder, not longer. Full plan: link in bio.

**Thread:**
1. How long should you hold a plank? Shorter than you think, and more than once.
2. The rule: only as long as you keep a straight line and breathe normally. When your hips sag or your back takes over, the set is over.
3. New to it: 3 holds of 10–20 seconds, on your knees if needed. Most people: 2–3 holds of 30–60 seconds (Mayo Clinic's range).
4. Spine researcher Stuart McGill argues for repeated ~10-second holds. His evidence is a small study, so don't treat 10 as magic. The takeaway holds either way: clean short holds beat one long sagging one.
5. That "average plank time" you saw? It came from college students holding until they quit, with a huge spread. Track your own clean time instead.
