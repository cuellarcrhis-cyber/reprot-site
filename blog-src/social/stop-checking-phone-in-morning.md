## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** The alarm goes off. Your thumb kills it. The phone is already unlocked. You know the rest.
2. **The real problem:** That reach isn't a decision. It's a setup: the phone is the alarm, it's 30 cm from your face, and it opens straight into everyone else's day.
3. **Tonight (3 minutes):** Charge it somewhere you have to walk to. Use a cheap alarm clock, or put the phone across the room. Leave a glass of water out.
4. **iPhone settings:** Scheduled Summary 30 min after you wake. Lock Screen set to Count. A "Morning" Focus with one plain Home Screen page.
5. **The myth:** "Your phone spikes your cortisol." No study shows that. A 2025 study of 201 adults found cortisol rises just as fast before waking as after.
6. **Save this: your first 10 minutes.** Feet on the floor. Water. Light. Ten easy squats (or sit-to-stand on the bed). Decide one thing. Then get the phone.
