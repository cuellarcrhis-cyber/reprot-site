## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Your Sleep Focus is on. Instagram still opens. Here's why.
2. **The difference:** Sleep Focus decides what can reach you. Downtime decides what you can open.
3. **The rename:** In iOS 27, Downtime is called Screen Time Schedule. Settings → Screen Time → Screen Time Schedule.
4. **The timing trick:** Sleep Focus starts at the beginning of Wind Down, not bedtime. Start your schedule at that same minute.
5. **Make it hold:** Screen Time passcode. Always Allowed for the people who need you. Share Across Devices so the iPad isn't the loophole.
6. **Save this:** Bedtime 11:00 + 45 min Wind Down = Sleep Focus and schedule both at 10:15. Schedule ends 30 min after your alarm.
7. **CTA:** Quiet phone and closed apps, at the same minute. Full steps for iOS 27 and iOS 26: link in bio.
