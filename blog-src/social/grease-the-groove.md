## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** The pull-up bar in your doorway is holding a tote bag. Here's how to use it without ever "working out."
2. **Grease the groove:** a few easy reps of one exercise, many times a day. Stop long before it gets hard.
3. **The rules:** about half your max per set. 4–8 sets a day, an hour or more apart. 5–6 days a week. Never to failure.
4. **Pick one or two moves:** pull-ups, push-ups or squats. Spread across six exercises and each gets too little practice.
5. **Can't do one yet?** Counter push-ups. Chair squats. Short dead hangs. Same rules.
6. **Save this:** Max 8 push-ups → sets of 4, five times a day. That's 20 a day, 100 a week, no workout.
7. **CTA:** The catch is remembering. Tie sets to the doorway, the kettle, your phone. Full plan and what the research does (and doesn't) say: link in bio.

2. The rules: about half your max per set, 4–8 sets a day, an hour or more apart, 5–6 days a week. Every set ends easy.
3. Pick one or two moves. Pull-ups and squats, or push-ups and squats. Can't do one rep? Counter push-ups, chair squats, short dead hangs.
4. Honest evidence check: no trial has tested the method itself. But a 2018 meta-analysis suggests training more often helps mostly by adding total reps, and a 2022 one found sets that stop short of failure built similar strength.
5. The real failure mode is forgetting. Attach sets to the doorway, the kettle, or the phone you pick up all day.
