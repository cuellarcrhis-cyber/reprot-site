## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Doing the 100 push-ups a day challenge? Check this number first.
2. **The test:** One set of floor push-ups, good form, stop when form breaks. That number decides your version.
3. **Your version:** 40+: go. 25–39: one easier week first. 10–24: a 4-week ramp. Under 10: 100 incline push-ups on a counter. All of them count.
4. **Split it:** Small sets across the day, each ending with two or three reps left. Ten sets of ten beats one ugly grind.
5. **Balance it:** One easy day a week. Rows under a sturdy table for your back. Change hand width and surface.
6. **Honest bit:** No study tests this challenge. Push-ups do build strength in small trials. 100 is just a round number.
7. **Save this:** Sharp joint pain, swelling, unusual weakness or dark urine means stop and see a doctor. Full plan at the link in bio.

**Caption:**
Most 100-a-day challenges end around day four, sore and sloppy. The fix isn't more willpower, it's picking the right starting point. Take one test set, find your version (from the full challenge to 100 incline push-ups), split the reps across the day and keep one easy day a week. We couldn't find any study of the challenge itself, so we stuck to what research on push-ups does show. If you have a shoulder, elbow or wrist injury, a heart condition or are pregnant, check with a doctor first. Full post: reprotapp.com/blog/100-push-ups-a-day
