**Thread:**
1. Buying a louder alarm is the most common fix for not getting out of bed. It almost never works. Here's why, and what does.
2. Right after waking you get sleep inertia: slower reactions, worse decisions. The Sleep Research Society says it usually fades within ~20 minutes, longer if you're short on sleep.
3. So the moment you decide "is getting up worth it?" is the moment your judgment is worst. The bed wins. That's a setup problem, not a character flaw.
4. Fix: decide the night before. Alarm across the room. First move chosen. One reason to be up (clothes out, coffee ready).
5. The 60-second routine: feet on the floor, stand to turn off the alarm, 10 squats (or chair sit-to-stands), light on, leave the room.
