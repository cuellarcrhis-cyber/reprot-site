## 1. Instagram carousel (8 slides)

1. **Hook:** Week 1: 3x10 squats, 8 push-ups. Week 6: 3x10 squats, 8 push-ups. That's why it "stopped working."
2. **The fix:** Bodyweight training works when the move keeps getting harder. Not more workouts. Harder rungs.
3. **The proof:** Small trials found progressive bodyweight squats and push-ups built strength about as well as barbell squats and bench press over 4–6 weeks.
   *Small samples (13 to 30 people). An early signal, not the last word.*
4. **The rule:** Start where you can do 6–8 clean reps. Hit 3 sets of 12? Move up one rung.
5. **Push ladder:** Wall → counter → knees → floor → feet raised.
6. **No pull-up bar?** Door-frame rows, then rows under a heavy, solid table. Test that it can't tip first.
7. **Save this week:** Mon / Wed / Fri, 25 minutes. Legs, push, pull, hips, trunk. 3 sets each, stop 1–3 reps short of failure. New? 2 sets, twice a week.
