## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Squats every day. Good idea or a fast way to wreck your legs?
2. **The answer:** Bodyweight squats daily are fine for most healthy people. The catch: most days should feel easy.
3. **Days 1–3:** Sore thighs. Normal. Soreness peaks 1–3 days after something new, then fades fast as your legs adapt.
4. **What the research says:** Total weekly work matters more than how many days you spread it over (2018 meta-analysis). Daily squats are just an easy way to get more practice in.
5. **Find your number:** Do one clean set. Under 5? Chair sit-to-stands. 15–30? 3 sets of 8–15. 50+? Split squats or a pause at the bottom.
6. **Take a day off when:** joint pain (not tired muscles), your form changes, or your test set drops two weeks in a row.
7. **Save this:** 15–60 good squats a day, 1–2 easy days a week, retest every 2 weeks. Full plan at the link in bio.
