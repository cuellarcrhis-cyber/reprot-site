## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** You don't need 30 plank variations. You need 3.
2. **Why groups:** A plank is a "don't move" exercise. Front, side and twist are three different jobs.
3. **Front:** Counter plank → knee plank → forearm plank. Move on at 3 steady holds of 20–30 s.
4. **Side:** Knee side plank → side plank from the feet. Start on your weaker side.
5. **Twist:** Shoulder taps from your knees → plank leg lifts. Slow, hips level.
6. **Save this:** Dead bug warm-up. 3 × your front plank. 2–3 × side plank per side. 2 × taps. Three days a week, about 10 minutes.
7. **CTA:** Harder, not longer. Full guide with every easier version: link in bio.

**Thread:**
1. You don't need 30 plank variations. You need one from each of three groups. Here's the beginner version, easiest first.
2. Front planks stop your back sagging. Counter plank → knee plank → forearm plank. Move on when 3 holds of 20–30 seconds feel steady.
3. Side planks are the ones beginners skip. Start on your knees, elbow under shoulder. Do your weaker side first and match it on the other.
4. Shoulder taps and leg lifts stop your hips twisting. From the knees or a counter first. Slow beats fast.
5. Easy at 45 seconds? Don't hold longer. In a small 2014 lab test, moving the elbows forward and squeezing the glutes lit up the abs more than a normal plank.
