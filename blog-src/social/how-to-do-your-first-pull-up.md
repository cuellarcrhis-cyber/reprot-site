# Distribution kit: How to Do Your First Pull-Up (An 8-Week Plan From Zero)

Post: https://reprotapp.com/blog/how-to-do-your-first-pull-up/

Notes: no "pull-up in 30 days" promises; the post says it can take weeks or
months. The Dayton study (Flanagan 2003) is small, older and in college-age
women; the "4 of 17" figure comes from press coverage. Never frame it as
"women can't do pull-ups" or tie it to body weight or appearance. The 2010
EMG study is 25 adults measuring muscle activity, not a training trial.
Always show the easier version (toes on a chair). Rep Rot counts the set;

## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Your pull-up bar has held more hoodies than people. Let's fix that.
2. **Why it's hard:** A pull-up lifts almost your whole body weight, with no easy setting built in. Can't do one? Normal.
3. **The ladder:** Dead hang → scapular pull → row → top hold → slow negative → assisted pull-up.
4. **Move-up tests:** 3 × 30 s hang. 3 × 8 scapular pulls. 3 × 3 five-second negatives. 3 × 5 assisted with little help.
5. **Chin-up first?** Palms facing you usually feels easier. Your first rep can be a chin-up.
6. **Save this plan:** 3 days a week, 15–20 minutes. Weeks 1–2 hangs and rows. 3–4 top holds. 5–6 negatives and assisted reps. 7–8 a fresh attempt.
7. **CTA:** Weeks or months, both are normal. Full plan at the link in bio.

## 3. X / Threads

**Standalone:**
You can't do a pull-up by trying a pull-up and failing every few weeks. Build it in pieces: hang, scapular pull, row, top hold, slow negative, assisted rep. Move up only when you pass each step's test. https://reprotapp.com/blog/how-to-do-your-first-pull-up/

**Thread:**
1. How to do your first pull-up from zero, without a gym. You need a bar and a sturdy chair.
2. Why it's hard: your back and arms lift almost your whole body weight, and unlike a push-up there's no easy setting built in.
3. The ladder: dead hang, scapular pull, row, top hold, slow 5-second negative, chair-assisted pull-up. Each one trains a piece of the full rep.
4. Timing, honestly: in one small 2003 training study, only 4 of 17 people got a full pull-up after about three months, even though everyone got stronger. Weeks or months are both normal.
5. Try a chin-up first. In a 2010 study of 25 adults, the biceps worked significantly harder with palms facing you, and for most people that makes it easier.
6. Tests for every step and an 8-week plan: https://reprotapp.com/blog/how-to-do-your-first-pull-up/
