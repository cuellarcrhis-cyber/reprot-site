## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** You don't need a living room. You need the strip of floor next to your bed.
2. **The rule:** 5 moves. 1 minute each. The bed is the only equipment.
3. **Minute 1:** Sit on the edge of the bed for 30 seconds. Stand up slowly. March in place.
   *Some people get lightheaded standing up after sleep. Sitting first is the move.*
4. **Minutes 2–3:** Squats to the bed (touch, stand). Incline push-ups on a sturdy frame or the wall. Not the mattress.
5. **Minutes 4–5:** Glute bridges on the floor. 20–30 second plank, then stand and reach.
6. **Save this:** Too much? Week 1: minutes 1–2 only. Add one minute a week. Missed a morning? Do the squats at lunch.
