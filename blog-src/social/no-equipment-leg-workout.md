Title: No-Equipment Leg Workout: Wall Sits, Squats or Lunges?

## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** No weights. 15 minutes. Squats, lunges or wall sits?
2. **The answer:** All three, in order. Squats first, a one-leg move next, a wall sit to finish.
3. **Squats:** The base. Most muscle, easiest to learn. Too easy? Slow the lowering to 3 seconds before adding reps.
4. **Lunges and split squats:** Not better than squats for strength in two small trials. They just stay hard longer at home, because one leg carries the load.
5. **Wall sits:** Gentle on knees that complain, and a good finisher. In a 270-trial review, holds like wall sits showed the biggest average drop in resting blood pressure. Breathe through them.
6. **The 15 minutes:** 3 sets of squats, 2 sets of split squats each leg, 2 wall sit holds. Starting out? Chair sit-to-stands, a counter to hold, a high wall sit.
7. **Save this:** 2–3 times a week. Stop for joint pain. Full plan by level at the link in bio.
