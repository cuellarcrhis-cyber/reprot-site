Title: Rest Days for Bodyweight Training: How Many Do You Need?

## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Push-ups every day, and your reps are going down. Do you need a rest day?
2. **The number:** Most people need 1–3 rest days a week. Beginners: train every other day.
3. **The real rule:** Rest the muscle, not the whole day. Give each muscle about two days between hard sessions.
4. **Bodyweight isn't "easy":** A set that leaves you shaking needs recovery, whether it's push-ups or a barbell.
5. **You don't need to go to failure:** A 2026 ACSM review of 137 reviews found failure didn't consistently improve results. Stop a couple of reps short and you recover faster.
6. **Rest day, done right:** A walk. Gentle stretching. 7+ hours of sleep. Not a "light" hour-long workout.
7. **Save this:** Take an extra day when reps drop twice in a row, your form changes, or a joint hurts. Three weekly plans at the link in bio.

**Thread:**
1. Your push-up reps went from 15 to 9 in a week of daily sets. That's not weakness. It's a missing rest day.
2. Most people need 1–3 rest days a week. Brand new? Train every other day.
3. Recovery is per muscle. Sore legs don't stop push-ups. Alternate push days and leg days and each muscle gets about two days off.
4. The 2026 ACSM position stand (137 reviews): train each muscle group at least twice a week. Going to failure didn't consistently help, and sets that stop short recover faster.
5. On rest days: walk, stretch gently, sleep 7+ hours. A meta-analysis found active recovery eased soreness, though not tiredness.
