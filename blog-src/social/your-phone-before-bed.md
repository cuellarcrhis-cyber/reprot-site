## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

**Thread:**
1. Most "phone before bed" advice is about blue light. The research points at something simpler: where you're using it.
2. A 2024 JAMA Pediatrics study put cameras on 11- to 14-year-olds. Screens in the 2 hours before bed: not linked to most sleep measures. Screens in bed: each extra 10 minutes of interactive use went with about 9 minutes less sleep.
3. Adults look similar. A 4-month study of 75 adults with sleep rings linked in-bed phone use to taking longer to fall asleep. Phone use outside bed wasn't.
4. And Night Shift? A 2021 BYU trial of 167 young adults found no sleep difference with it on, off, or with no phone at all.
5. So the rule: the phone can be part of your evening, not part of your bed. Charge it outside the bedroom; buy a cheap alarm clock.
