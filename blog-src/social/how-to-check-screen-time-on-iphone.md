# Distribution kit: How to Check Screen Time on iPhone (and What the Numbers Mean)

Post: https://reprotapp.com/blog/how-to-check-screen-time-on-iphone/

Notes: no shaming, no "you're addicted", no invented "average person" stats.
The 19-hours figure is an average misestimate in one study of 325 iPhone
users (2020, cross-sectional); never say "everyone underestimates by 19
hours" or that it proves anything about mental health. There is no official
"too much" number for adults; don't invent one. Rep Rot doesn't show usage
reports; don't imply it does.

## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** You think it's 2 hours. Your iPhone has the real number.
2. **Where it is (iOS 27):** Settings → Screen Time → Daily Average → Week. On iOS 26: See All App & Website Activity.
3. **Pickups:** how many times you woke the phone. Not time. High pickups, short sessions = reflex checking.
4. **Notifications:** how many you received, by app. The top one is setting your schedule.
5. **The trap:** with Share Across Devices on, your total can include your iPad and Mac.
6. **Save this week:** Day 1, write your guess. Days 1–6, change nothing. Day 7, compare, note top app, worst hour, loudest app. Pick one change.
7. **CTA:** Full guide, with what the research says about our guesses: link in bio.
