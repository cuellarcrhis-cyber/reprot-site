## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** How many push-ups a day? Not the number from the challenge video.
2. There's no study that found one daily number for everyone. Your number starts with one test.
3. **The test:** warm up, then one set of floor push-ups. Stop when your form breaks, not when you collapse. Write it down.
4. **The rule of thumb:** sets of about half that number, 2–4 sets, a few reps left in the tank.
5. **Can't do one from the floor?** Hands on a counter. Then a bed. Then the floor. Lower the surface as you get stronger.
6. **Save this:** 0 → 3×5–10 on a counter. 1–5 → 3×5–8 on a bench. 6–15 → 3×3–8. 16–30 → 3–4×8–15. 30+ → 3–5 sets of half your max. Retest every two weeks.
7. **CTA:** Daily is fine at moderate effort; take a day or two off a week. Full guide, and what the "40 push-ups" study actually found, at the link in bio.
