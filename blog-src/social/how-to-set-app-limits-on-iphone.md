## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Your iPhone app limit isn't broken. It's built to be skipped.
2. Apple's own guide: by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once you reach them. One tap and you're back in.
3. Where they went in iOS 27: Settings → Screen Time → Time Allowances. (iOS 26: Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit.)
4. The setting that makes a limit hold: a Screen Time passcode. Settings → Screen Time → Passcode & Management → Lock Screen Time Settings.
5. Make it harder to cheat yourself: have a friend type the code. Limit the whole category, not one app. Add a schedule for your worst hour.
6. **Save this:** Start at about half your real daily average. 3+ hours → 90 min. 2 hours → 60. 1 hour → 30. Step down after a couple of weeks.
7. **CTA:** Full setup, plus what the research says limits actually do, at the link in bio.
