Notes: the force numbers come from small studies (Ebben 2011, 23 adults;

## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Can't do one push-up? You're not weak. You're lifting too much.
2. A floor push-up puts roughly two-thirds of your body weight on your hands. Hands raised about 2 feet: around 41%.
3. **Your push-up ladder:** wall → counter → table → sofa seat → stair → floor.
4. **The test:** find the lowest rung where you can do 8 clean reps. Straight line head to heels, all the way down.
5. **3 days a week:** 3 sets of 8–12 on your rung, slow 3–5 second negatives, short planks.
6. **Save this rule:** every set clean? Move down one rung. Not yet? Repeat the week. That's still progress.
7. **CTA:** Full 4-week plan, form fixes and FAQ at the link in bio.
