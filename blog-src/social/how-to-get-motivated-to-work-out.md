**Thread:**
1. "How do I get motivated to work out?" The honest answer: stop trying to get motivated first. Here's what the research says.
2. A 2013 meta-analysis: nearly half of people who intended to be active didn't follow through. Most of the gap was people who wanted to.
3. A 2016 diary study split exercise habits into starting vs. doing. Only the starting habit predicted how often people exercised.
4. Most people feel better once moderate exercise is underway. Push past the point where you can't talk, and pleasure drops. Keep the first minutes easy.
5. A megastudy of 61,293 gym members: most motivation programs faded once they ended. The best one rewarded coming back after a missed workout.
6. So: a 2-minute start, a specific cue, easy first minutes, and coming back after a miss. Full post and sources: reprotapp.com/blog/how-to-get-motivated-to-work-out/
