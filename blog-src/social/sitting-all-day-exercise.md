2. A 2023 Columbia trial had 11 adults sit for 8 hours under different walking schedules. Five minutes of walking every 30 minutes was the only one that lowered both blood sugar and blood pressure. Tiny study, one day at a time. But consistent with older work.
3. Does a workout cancel out sitting? Partly. A pooled analysis of 44,370 adults found 30–40 minutes a day of brisk activity weakened the link between sitting and death risk. Observational, and "weakened" isn't "erased." You want both.
