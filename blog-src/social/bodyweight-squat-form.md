## 1. Instagram carousel (7 slides)

1. **Hook:** Ten squats while the kettle boils. Rep six looked nothing like rep one.
2. **The six steps:** Feet slightly wider than shoulders. Brace. Sit back and down. Knees over toes. As low as you can control. Stand up through your whole foot.
3. **Mistake 1–2:** Knees caving in? Widen your stance, "spread the floor." Heels lifting? Sit back more, squat to a chair until they stay down.
4. **Mistake 3–5:** Back rounding? Stop just above it. Chest dropping? Arms forward, eyes up. Half reps? Use a chair so you know where the bottom is.
5. **The myth:** Knees past your toes is usually fine. Knees caving inward is the thing to fix.
6. **Save this ladder:** Chair sit-to-stand → hold a counter → touch a chair → full squat → 3 seconds down. Move up at 12 clean reps.
7. **CTA:** Film three reps from the side and front with your phone. Full checklist and fixes: link in bio.

## 3. X / Threads

**Standalone:**

**Thread:**
1. Most people were never taught a squat. Here's the 30-second version.
2. Feet slightly wider than shoulders. Brace your stomach. Sit back and down, hips and knees together. Knees over toes. As low as you can control. Stand up through the whole foot.
3. The five usual mistakes: knees caving in, heels lifting, back rounding, chest dropping, half reps. Each has a simple fix.
4. Knees past toes? In a small 2003 study, blocking the knees shifted load to the hips and made people lean forward. Where your knees point matters more than how far they go.
5. Can't do a full squat yet? Sit down on a chair and stand up without your hands. That counts. Work up from there.
