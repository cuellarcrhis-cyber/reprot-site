## Search intent

Someone wants a concrete daily number they can start with today, and to know
whether doing them every day is OK.

## Must-cover

- Sets/reps by level (beginner, intermediate)

- The "40 push-ups" heart-health study, honestly
- How to progress

## Questions people ask

- Is it bad to do push-ups every day? (Answer as an h2.)
- How many push-ups should I be able to do? (Answer with the Yang study and its limits; no invented norms.)

## Our edge

1. A test-then-prescribe method: do one honest max set, then a daily number that's about half of it, split into sets. A table by max.
2. Honest evidence: Yang 2019 (1,104 male firefighters, observational, >40 vs <10 linked to 96% lower CVD event rate; not generalizable; not a target), Kikuchi 2017 and Calatayud 2015 (push-ups can build strength comparably to bench press in small trials), US guidelines (2+ days/week of muscle strengthening, all major groups).
3. Scale-downs for every level (wall, counter, knees) and clear safety notes.
4. A "push-up day vs. rest day" weekly template.
