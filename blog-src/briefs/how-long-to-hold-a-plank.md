## Search intent

A number: how many seconds to hold a plank for their level, and when to stop.

## Must-cover

- A time range by level (beginner vs. experienced)
- "Form over time": what breaking form looks like
- Short repeated holds vs. one long hold (McGill)
- Easier versions (knees, incline) and harder ones
- Progression and how often to plank

## Questions people ask

- Is a 1-minute plank good? (Yes, solid; beyond ~60 s make it harder, not longer.)
- What's the average plank time? (Strand 2014 means, with caveats.)
- Should you plank every day? (Easy holds most days are fine; PAG 2+ days/week for strength.)

## Our edge

## Sources
