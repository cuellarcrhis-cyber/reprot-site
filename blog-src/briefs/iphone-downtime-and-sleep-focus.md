## Search intent

Someone wants their iPhone to stop tempting them at night and wants to know
whether Downtime, Sleep Focus or both does that, with current steps.

## Must-cover

- What Downtime does (blocks apps except Always Allowed) vs what Sleep Focus does (filters notifications, can swap Lock/Home Screen)
- iOS 27 rename: Downtime → Screen Time Schedule
- Setting the sleep schedule and Wind Down in Health
- Customizing Sleep Focus (People, Apps, Home Screen)
- Always Allowed / who can still reach you
- Passcode so it can't be switched off; Block at Downtime on iOS 26
- Troubleshooting: why downtime doesn't work

## Questions people ask

- Does Sleep Focus block apps? (No: it filters notifications and can show a pared-down Home Screen; apps still open. Blocking is Screen Time.)
- Do Downtime and Sleep Focus turn on together? (No: Sleep Focus follows the Health sleep schedule, starting at Wind Down; the Screen Time Schedule has its own times. Line them up by hand.)
- Why is Downtime not working? (No passcode; iOS 26 Block at Downtime off; the app is in Always Allowed; schedule on wrong days; other devices not sharing settings.)

## Our edge

1. One clear rule: Sleep Focus decides what can reach you; the Schedule decides what you can open. A side-by-side table.
2. Current iOS 27 steps verified against Apple, with an iOS 26 fallback (Block at Downtime, 5-min reminder).
3. A worked timeline (bedtime 11:00, Wind Down 45 min → both start 10:15, schedule ends after wake-up).
4. Honest limits (Apple doesn't document whether iOS 27 schedules can be dismissed; test it), privacy note on Focus status sharing, and a short evidence section (in-bed use, regular sleep times) without promises.

## Sources

- Apple, Set Screen Time Schedules and Time Allowances (iOS 27): Settings → Screen Time → Screen Time Schedule → Schedule → Add Schedule → days → Time Window (name, start/stop, Time Allowance categories, Always Allowed). Needs iOS 27 etc. on all devices. Always Allowed: Settings → Screen Time → Always Allowed → Add People / Add Apps.
- Apple, Set up Screen Time for yourself (iOS 27): Share Across Devices applies schedules to iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- Apple, Choose who you can communicate with at certain times (iOS 27): Communication Limits → When Device Paused or Unavailable → Always Allowed or Everyone.
- Apple, Create a Screen Time passcode: Passcode & Management → Lock Screen Time Settings; Passcode Recovery.
- Apple, Set schedules with Screen Time (iOS 26): Downtime → Scheduled, Every Day / Customize Days; 5-min reminder; by default limits can be ignored; Block at Downtime needs a passcode; apps dimmed with hourglass.
- Apple, Set up sleep in Health: Wind Down 15 min to 3 hours; Sleep Focus begins at start of Wind Down; Settings → Focus → Sleep to customize.
- Apple, Edit sleep schedules: Health → Browse → Sleep → Sleep → Full Schedule & Options → Wind Down.
- Apple, Turn Sleep Focus on or off: Control Center; Use Schedule for Sleep Focus.
- Apple, Set up a Focus: People, Apps, Options, Lock/Home Screen, Focus filters; Share Focus Status shows others you've silenced notifications.
- Apple, Turn on or schedule a Focus: Sleep Focus follows the Health sleep schedule.
- Brosnan et al. 2024 JAMA Pediatrics (via Otago): in-bed screen use linked to less sleep (ages 11–14, observational).
- He et al. 2020 PLoS ONE pilot RCT (38 students, no phone 30 min before bed).
- Windred et al. 2023, Sleep (UK Biobank, 60,977 adults, mean age ~63, 7 days of accelerometry; more regular sleep linked to 20–48% lower all-cause mortality risk; observational).
