## Search intent

Someone heard about grease the groove and wants to know exactly how to do it today: how many reps per set, how many sets, how often, and whether it works.

## Must-cover

- What it is and who coined it
- Per-set size (about half your max) and never to failure
- Sets per day, spacing, days per week
- Which exercises work (pull-ups, push-ups, squats; one or two moves)
- Limits: not ideal for building size, not a full program
- Pull-ups as the classic example

## Our edge

1. Honest evidence: no trial of GTG itself; what nearby research says (frequency meta-analysis, failure vs non-failure meta-analysis), with caveats.
2. A rep table by max for push-ups, pull-ups and squats, with easier versions.
3. The real problem is remembering: tie sets to triggers you already hit (doorway, kettle, app limit) — a plan that fits phone-heavy days.
4. Safety: doorway bar checks, stop-before-form-breaks, doctor note.
