## Search intent

Someone wants a checklist for one good bodyweight squat they can do right now,
and to know if what they're doing (heels up, knees in, knees past toes) is wrong.

## Must-cover

- Stance and foot position; hips and knees bend together; depth
- Knees tracking over toes; knees caving in
- Heels lifting; back rounding; chest dropping; breathing
- Beginner reps/sets; easier versions (chair squat, holding a counter)

## Questions people ask

- Is it bad if my knees go past my toes? (Not for most people; Fry 2003, Mayo.)
- How deep should I squat? (As low as you can with good form; Hartmann 2013 review.)
- Why do my heels come up? / How many squats should a beginner do?

## Our edge

1. A mistake → cause → fix table you can use mid-set.
2. Honest take on "knees past toes" using the Fry 2003 abstract (7 trained men, barbell) and Mayo's cue, with caveats.
3. A five-step scale-down ladder from chair sit-to-stand to a slow full squat.
