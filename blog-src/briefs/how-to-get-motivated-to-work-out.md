## Our edge

1. Honest framing of "motivation follows action": what's shown (the deciding-to-start habit predicts frequency; most people feel better during easy-to-moderate exercise) and what isn't (no trial proves starting creates motivation).
2. A 2-minute start routine with a table by situation and easier versions, at home, no gear.
3. The megastudy and bundling evidence told with its decay: most boosts faded when the program ended; rewards for coming back after a miss did best.
4. A careful low-mood section with a support line.

## Facts used (verified via search summaries)

- Rhodes RE, de Bruijn GJ, Br J Health Psychol 2013 (PubMed 23480428): meta-analysis of the physical activity intention-behaviour gap; overall gap 46% in the abstract (48% elsewhere in the paper), mostly from intenders who don't act. Post says "nearly half".
- Phillips LA, Gardner B, Health Psychology 2016, 35(1):69-77 (doi 10.1037/hea0000249): ~123 adults, 1-month diaries; instigation habit strength was the only unique predictor of exercise frequency, execution habit was not.
- Reed J, Ones DS, Psychology of Sport and Exercise 2006, 7(5): meta-analysis (158 studies per secondary sources) — a typical acute aerobic bout raises self-reported positive activated (energetic) feelings; control conditions lower them; low intensity did best. Post says "about 150 studies".
- Ekkekakis P, Parfitt G, Petruzzello SJ, Sports Medicine 2011 (PubMed 21780850): review of 33 studies; pleasure mainly drops above the ventilatory/lactate threshold; sub-threshold intensity feels pleasant for most.
- Rhodes RE, Kates A, Ann Behav Med 2015 (doi 10.1007/s12160-015-9704-5): review of 24 studies; a positive change in feeling during moderate exercise was linked to future activity; post-exercise feelings were not (only 4 studies on in-exercise affect).
- Milkman KL et al., Nature 2021, 600:478-483: 61,293 gym members, 54 four-week programs; ~45% raised visits (9–27%); top one gave small rewards for returning after a missed workout; only ~8% had measurable effects after the program ended.
- Milkman KL, Minson JA, Volpp KGM, Management Science 2014 (PubMed 25843979): 226 people; gym-only audiobooks raised visits 51% at first, effect faded over time (after Thanksgiving).
- Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, 2nd ed.: 150 min moderate aerobic + 2 days muscle-strengthening; 10-minute minimum bout removed.
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): call or text 988.

## Product signal

The best-performing arm in the 61,293-person megastudy rewarded people for returning after a missed workout, and Phillips & Gardner found the "start" cue, not the workout itself, predicts frequency. Rep Rot's trigger (app limit / alarm) is already a start cue, and "come back after a miss" sharpens the existing streak/recovery thinking already logged (2026-11-05 notes, how-to-stay-consistent). Evidence is search-summary only and it isn't a specific new screen change.
