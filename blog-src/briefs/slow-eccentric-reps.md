## Our edge

1. The study read honestly: 22 sedentary adults, 32–69, mostly women, 4 weeks, before/after with a 2-week control period, big average gains with huge spread, no change in body composition, BP or resting HR.
2. Tempo evidence: rep durations 0.5–8 s grow muscle similarly (Schoenfeld 2015, 8 studies); >10 s looked worse. So 3–5 s is a tool for beginners and short sessions, not magic.
3. A printable 5-minute routine with an easier and a harder version of each move.
4. Consistency angle with evidence: a small number of eccentric contractions daily beat a bigger weekly dose (Yoshida 2022).
